# PostQueue

A Windows and Mac app that posts your photos and videos to Instagram on a schedule. Drop files in, pick the date and time for each, write the caption, and PostQueue publishes them through Meta's official Instagram API while it sits in the notification area or menu bar.

- [User guide](USER_GUIDE.md): install, one-time setup, daily use.
- [Picture by picture](walkthrough/README.md): every screen from first launch to a posted photo.
- [Privacy policy](privacy.md): what the app keeps, where, and what it sends.
- [Delete your data](data-deletion.md): how to remove everything PostQueue kept.
